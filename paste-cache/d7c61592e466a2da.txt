Two things to watch out for, both from upstream:

1. Right-click copies instead of pasting. Standard Windows terminal muscle memory pastes on right-click. Here it copies the selection, and there's no right-click paste — use ALT+v or CTRL+SHIFT+V.
2. SHIFT+middle-click kills a pane with confirm=false. No dialog, no undo. Easy to hit by accident while scrolling.
