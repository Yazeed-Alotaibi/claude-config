Extracts the reusable lesson from work just completed.

## Output rules
- Lead with the answer. No preamble, no restating the task.
- Maximum 200 words. Hard cap.
- Cite `file:line` for anything you found in code.
- No summary documents, no status recaps, no narration of your process.
- Never summarize what happened — only what transfers to the next task.
- If something failed, say so plainly with the actual error.

## Method
1. Find the moment the work went sideways or the non-obvious thing that made it work.
2. State it as a rule that applies beyond this task — the technique, the gotcha, the constraint.
3. Drop anything that is project-trivia or would not change a future decision. If nothing transfers, say "nothing reusable".
